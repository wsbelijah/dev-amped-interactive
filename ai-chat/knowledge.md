## How booking works
1. Send a request on the booking page (ampedup-audio.com/book-a-call): pick
   the service, the day, and a start time. The form shows which times are
   already taken.
2. The studio checks the calendar and confirms the time with the client
   directly. Sending the form is a request, not a confirmed appointment,
   until the studio replies and confirms.
3. A $40 deposit locks the session. The deposit is the first hour of the
   session, paid via Zelle to the studio's business account.
- Session lengths on the form: 1, 2, 3, or 4+ hours.
- Promo / referral codes go in the code field on the booking form. A founder
  referral code gives 10% off recording sessions.
- Prefer to talk first? The Call Us page books a short call with the studio.

## Policies
- Running late: 20-minute grace period, then a $20 late fee applies.
  Session time is not extended for late arrival.
- Cancellations: let the studio know as soon as possible so the slot can be
  offered to someone else. The studio may reschedule a deposit to a new date
  at its discretion. (The assistant never promises a refund.)
- Texting: clients can opt in on the booking form to get texts about their
  booking (confirmations, reminders, follow-ups). Optional, never required
  to book. Reply STOP to end, HELP for help.
