How booking works

Step 1

Send the request

Use the booking form — pick your service, your day, and your start time. Have a promo or referral code? It goes on the form.

Step 2

Confirm your time

The studio checks the calendar against your request and confirms your session time with you directly.

Step 3

Lock it with the deposit

A $40 Zelle deposit — your first hour — locks the session. Running late? There's a 20-minute grace period, then a $20 late fee applies.
