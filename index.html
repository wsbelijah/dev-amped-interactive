FAQ

Before you book

How do I lock in a session?

Send a booking request on the booking page. A $40 deposit via Zelle — your first hour — locks your time on the calendar.

What happens if I'm running late?

You get a 20-minute grace period. After that, a $20 late fee applies.

I have a promo or referral code — where does it go?

Drop it in the promo / referral code field on the booking form and it will be applied to your request.

Where is the studio?

AMPED UP AUDIO is in El Monte, California. The exact location and parking details come with your booking confirmation.

What happens after I send a booking request?

Your request goes straight to the studio. You'll hear back to confirm your time, and your $40 Zelle deposit locks it in.

How much does a recording session cost?

Recording is $40/hour, with an engineer included for your whole session — no separate engineer fee and no packages. A $40 deposit (your first hour) locks a confirmed session.

How much does mixing cost?

Mixing is separate from recording and priced flat: $100 for a 2-track mix, $150 for a full stem mix.

What hours is the studio open?

Monday 12 PM–2 AM, Tuesday 5 PM–2 AM, Wednesday and Thursday 7 PM–2 AM, and Friday–Sunday 9 AM–3 PM and 9 PM–2 AM.
